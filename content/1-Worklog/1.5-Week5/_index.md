### Week 5 Achievements:

- Implemented a PoC re-ranker (Cross-Encoder) and measured its initial effect on retrieval quality and latency; identified further optimization opportunities (batching, FP16) to reduce latency.
- Integrated Re-ranking into the pipeline as a toggleable module, returning ranking metadata (ranking score, normalized_score) to support improved context selection for the AI model.
- Finalized Google Sign-in: OAuth 2.0 with Cognito + Google works reliably; added account-linking logic to handle email collisions.
- Completed the backend profile work: profile APIs, avatar handling, and password-change flows are stable; synchronization between Cognito, DynamoDB and S3 was optimized.
- Performed comparative tests between Standard RAG, Self-RAG and Re-ranking on a sample dataset; collected metrics (precision@k, answer F1, latency, confidence calibration) to guide next steps.
- Ran team review meetings and participated in the "FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week powered by GenAI Fund" event (25 July 2026).
